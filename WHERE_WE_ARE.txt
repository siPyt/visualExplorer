ULTIMATE MISSION
----------------
Walk up to ANY Emerson DeltaV Control Studio workstation at ANY plant, open ANY
module (CM, EM, or EPH — Off-Line or On-Line), and immediately read what every
block, wire, pin, mode, status, and interlock is doing like the sharpest
engineer in the room.

HOW THIS WORKSPACE OPERATES
---------------------------
1. Visual Explorer (index.html):
   The interactive visual Control Studio trainer. Switch modules in the top-left
   picker (EM_TANK_CHARGE + EM_XFER_HDR) to inspect authentic DeltaV Control
   Studio stencils, wires, pins, On-Line live values, and fault simulations.
2. Running Study Handbook (DeltaV_Study_Guide.pdf):
   Print-ready study guide + plain-English DeltaV vocabulary + self-check quizzes.
3. Session Tracker (WHERE_WE_ARE.txt — this file):
   Single source of truth so even a brand-new chat instantly knows where we came
   from, where we are right now, and where we are going next.

1. WHERE WE CAME FROM (MASTERED SO FAR)
================================================================================
[DONE] Ch. 1 — Equipment Hierarchy & Scan Heartbeat
       - EPH (Recipe Boss) -> EM (Team Coordinator) -> CM (Single-Device Controller).
       - Module Scan cycle and #1 -> #2 -> #3 FBD execution order.

[DONE] Ch. 2 — 1 Outer EM Algorithm vs. 4 Inner Patterns
       - 1 Outer Algorithm chosen at creation: Command-Driven, State-Driven, FBD, SFC.
       - 4 Inner Patterns inside EM_TANK_CHARGE (Pattern 1 SFC + Patterns 2–4 FBD).
       - Solid wires = Analog (.CV numbers); Dashed wires = Discrete / Mode / Status.

[DONE] Ch. 3 — Operating Modes & Signal Health
       - OOS, MAN, AUTO, CAS; Write MODE.TARGET, always verify MODE.ACTUAL.
       - Every wire carries .CV (value) + .ST (GOOD / UNCERTAIN / BAD).

[DONE] Ch. 4 — 5-Second DeltaV Path Decoding
       - 'Module/Block/Parameter.Detail' and '^/' local shortcut, Named Sets.

[DONE] Ch. 5 — Hold, Restart, Abort, Golden Tare Rule & EPH Handshake
       - Golden Tare Rule: Snapshot START_WT.CV once on S0_IDLE -> S10_CHARGE_OPEN;
         NEVER overwrite START_WT.CV on Restart from Hold (prevents overfill).
       - EPH <-> EM 3-signal handshake: Acquire, Command + Setpoint down, EM_STATE up.

[DONE] Ch. 6 — 30-Second On-Line Troubleshooting Method
       - Read order: Pattern 1 Active Step -> Transition Crossbar (┼) -> FBD Pattern.
       - FAIL_ACTIVE mismatch alarms & CND DURATION confirm timers.

[DONE] Ch. 7 — State-Driven Equipment Modules (EM_XFER_HDR)
       - Command-Driven (multi-step task over time) vs. State-Driven (direct snap to
         a Named-Set alignment mask for headers & transfer manifolds).
       - EM_S_COMMAND (desired state) vs. PV_D (confirmed state from all CM switches).
       - Anti-cross-mix pump gating (CND_PATH_OK holds CM_XFER_PMP stopped until all
         isolation valves match mask), FAIL_STATE fallback on overpressure, and
         FAIL_ACTIVE transition timeout alarm.
       - Added live EM_XFER_HDR module (3 tabs + fault simulator) to Visual Explorer!
