1. WHERE WE CAME FROM (MASTERED SO FAR)
================================================================================
[DONE] Ch. 1 — Equipment Hierarchy & Scan Heartbeat
       - EPH (Recipe Boss) -> EM (Team Coordinator) -> CM (Single-Device Controller).
       - Module Scan cycle and #1 -> #2 -> #3 FBD execution order.

[DONE] Ch. 2 — 1 Outer EM Algorithm vs. 4 Inner Patterns
       - 1 Outer Algorithm chosen at creation: Command-Driven, State-Driven, FBD, SFC.
       - 4 Inner Patterns inside EM_TANK_CHARGE:
         * Pattern 1 (SFC, top-to-bottom): ONLY pattern that steps through time.
         * Pattern 2 (FBD, left-to-right): Permissives (pre-start) & Interlocks (trips).
         * Pattern 3 (FBD, left-to-right): Weight selection (BSEL), Tare math (CALC),
           Cutoff check (CND), Mode push (ACT).
         * Pattern 4 (FBD, left-to-right): CM commands (CAS_IN_D) & field proof (PV_D).
       - Solid wires = Analog (.CV numbers); Dashed wires = Discrete / Mode / Status.

[DONE] Ch. 3 — Operating Modes & Signal Health
       - OOS, MAN, AUTO, CAS.
       - Write MODE.TARGET, always verify MODE.ACTUAL.
       - Every wire carries .CV (value) + .ST (GOOD / UNCERTAIN / BAD).

[DONE] Ch. 4 — 5-Second DeltaV Path Decoding
       - 'Module/Block/Parameter.Detail' (e.g., 'CM_FEED_XV/DC1/MODE.ACTUAL' = CAS).
       - '^/' shortcut for local module parameters, Named Sets, unquoted mode keywords.

[DONE] Ch. 5 — Hold, Restart, Abort, Golden Tare Rule & EPH Handshake
       - Golden Tare Rule: Snapshot START_WT.CV once on S0_IDLE -> S10_CHARGE_OPEN;
         NEVER overwrite START_WT.CV on Restart from Hold (prevents overfill).
       - EPH <-> EM 3-signal handshake: Acquire, Command + Setpoint down, EM_STATE up.

[DONE] Ch. 6 — 30-Second On-Line Troubleshooting Method
       - Read order: Pattern 1 Active Step -> Transition Crossbar (┼) -> FBD Pattern.
       - Diagnosing stuck-in-Idle (MODE.ACTUAL != CAS), mid-run Hold (OUT.ST = BAD),
         and stuck valve in S20_TRIM_CLOSE (CAS_IN_D vs. PV_D mismatch, DURATION,
         FAIL_ACTIVE).
